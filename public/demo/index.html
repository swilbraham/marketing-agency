How it works

Three steps for your customer

The same satellite measuring method an estimator uses on site — just done by the customer.

1

They find their home

A postcode drops the map straight onto their property in high-resolution satellite view.

2

They trace the area

Click each corner to draw round it. Extra shapes cover a garage, extension or second patio.

3

You get the enquiry

Name, phone, address and the exact measurement land in your inbox, with the price they saw.

Who it suits

Any job you price by the square metre

If your quote starts with "how big is it?", this does the measuring for you. Each service gets its own rate and minimum charge, and sloped surfaces are handled properly — a roof seen from above is smaller than the roof you actually work on, so the pitch is factored in.

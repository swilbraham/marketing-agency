How the calculator works

Three steps. About a minute.

It uses the same satellite imagery and measuring method a surveyor would use on site.

1

Find your home

Type your postcode or full address. The map drops straight onto your roof in high-resolution satellite view.

2

Trace the area

Click each corner of the roof, drive or patio to draw round it. Add extra shapes for a garage, extension or a second patio.

3

See your price

For roofs we allow for the pitch; drives and patios are measured flat. Multiply by the rate and your price appears instantly.
